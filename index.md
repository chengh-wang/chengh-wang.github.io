I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Engineering at Northeastern University, advised by Prof. Alireza Ramezani. My research interests lie at the intersection of robotics, machine learning, and hardware — particularly policy design that enables polyarticular mechanisms to achieve agile physical behavior. My current research focuses on humanoid whole-body loco-manipulation and dexterous manipulation. My long-term goal is to develop intelligent and robust control methods that enable robots to achieve human-level agility and dexterity.
